## Problem

VELOVE Uni ZH runs free repair sessions for students. The old website was hard to update, slow on
phones and did not make the most important information — _when and where is the next repair
session?_ — easy to find. Volunteers change every semester, so the site has to be maintainable by
people who are not developers.

## Approach

- **Content first:** interviewed volunteers and visitors, then restructured the site around three
  tasks: find the next session, learn what can be repaired, get involved.
- **Next.js with static generation** for speed and zero-cost hosting; session dates come from a
  single data file that volunteers can edit through GitHub's web editor.
- **Accessible, mobile-first design** with large tap targets, high contrast and bilingual content.

## Result

The new site is in progress. {/* TODO */} The first version loads in under a second on mobile and
costs nothing to host. Next steps: a simple booking flow and an editing guide for future
